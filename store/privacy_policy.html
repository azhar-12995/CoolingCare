Cooling Care — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 09 October 2026

Your account

You log in with your mobile number. Google Firebase Authentication sends a one-time SMS code to verify it. Your name, phone number, area and address are stored in Cooling Care’s Firebase database (Google Cloud, region asia-south1) so our team can reach you and send the technician to the right place.

Orders

Each order (service, appliance, date and time, address, contact number, the issue you describe and its status history) is stored so Cooling Care can plan the visit and you can follow it in the app. Only you and the Cooling Care team can see your orders. Ratings you give are saved on the order.

Camera and photos

Photos you add are uploaded with your order to Firebase Storage so the technician can see the problem. Only you and the Cooling Care team can open them. The app does not access your gallery except for the photos you pick.

Notifications

We use Firebase Cloud Messaging to tell you when your visit is confirmed, when the technician leaves and when the work is done. Visit and maintenance reminders are scheduled on your phone. You can turn them off in Settings or in your phone’s notification settings.

Sharing

We do not sell or share your data with anyone. Google Firebase processes it on our behalf as a service provider. When you choose to chat with us on WhatsApp, that conversation is handled under WhatsApp’s own privacy policy.

No tracking

The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking libraries and does not use your location.

Delete your account

Settings › Delete account removes your profile and your login. Past orders are kept by Cooling Care for its service records; ask us on WhatsApp or by phone to remove them too and we will do so.
